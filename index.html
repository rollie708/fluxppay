Questions, answered

Before you
get started.

What is FluxP?+

FluxP is a product concept focused on making crypto payments clearer and easier for products, services, online stores, and digital businesses.

Can I make a real payment right now?+

No. The current website is an interactive product preview. Real payment functionality is not yet available.

Does the demo connect to my wallet?+

No. The demo is self-contained and frontend-only. It never connects to a wallet, requests a signature, or transfers funds.

What cryptocurrencies will FluxP support?+

Supported assets and networks are still being explored. The options in this preview use sample conversion values only.

Who is FluxP being built for?+

FluxP is being shaped for people paying online and for stores, freelancers, and digital businesses requesting payment.
